## Scintilla's targets, 2026-11-27, 13:43 to 14:31

**For research's pick, one fall and one burn.** Sheets in `build/handoff/ShadeFire/targets/`: `<look>_sheet.png` (the peak, the whole frame by day and at night; today's beside the target, close; the strip by day and at night; 64 px over dark; for the burns, five headings at 64 px) and `<look>_peak_{day,night}.png`.
- **Fall A, the break** (`fall_a`, peak 0.10 s): a fireball where the foe stood, swelling to 1.6 times the body, with a white-hot middle. Licks rise off its top, gold chips fly out with the blow, and a ring of light crosses the floor. It cools and rises into a soft puff, gone by 0.6 s.

- **Burn A, the tongues** (`burn_a`, peak 0.16 s): eight flame tongues on the shoulders, the arms' backs, the back and the hips, each on its own beat (0.33 to 0.58 s), plus embers. Nothing rises from the head or neck.
- **Burn B, the wrap** (`burn_b`, peak 0.16 s): two strands of flame wound round the body outside the arms, at different heights and climbs, turning on a 0.56 s loop, with licks flickering up off them, plus embers. An earlier take read as a grinning face from behind, because of shoulder tongues over the strands' lower arc. Those tongues are gone, and the strands no longer line up.

Fall A's middle is an additive glow (in the game, `fx_glow` on one particle). The smoke is soft-edged, in two tones.
