# AR75. The whole frame: the fight HUD and the base, one composition (research's call; Modulo, research, then Faber and Tessera)

**The director, 2026-11-28, by 00:20,** on a phone capture of a fight in the base: "the whole hud and base, map looks like a hot mess of garbage ik it a prototype but we are going very bad right now." This replaces AR74 (the HUD's left cluster alone). Fixing one cluster inside a broken frame is the piece-by-piece approach that produced the frame.

- **What the capture shows (research):**
  - **Five visual languages in one frame,** none designed with the others: slanted dark plates (the lead), a red framed bar (the foe), a purple world sign (VOID BREAKER), a teal panel with a cream button (While you were away), and a white tile and ring under the player.
  - **The world's labels sit in the HUD's layer:** VOID BREAKER covers the foe's bar, and Block and the damage numbers land on top of it.
  - **The base is a pale, empty plane** with a grey wall, a flat sky and machines standing apart.
  - **No hierarchy:** every element is as loud as the fight.
- **The cause:** each piece was designed and passed alone, and nobody owned the whole frame.
- **Feel line:** on a phone in a fight at the base, the eye goes to the fight first, then to the lead's health and the signature, and everything else is quiet. One visual language, painted and premium beside the anime games on Roblox's front page.
- **Order:**
  1. **The composition** (Modulo, now): two or three layouts of the whole 16:9 phone frame in grey, saying what sits where, its size and its priority, each beside a shipped HUD at the same size. Research picks (the UI is the team's, 2026-11-27, 13:36).
  2. **The look** (research, through rule 2's AI art): two or three painted whole frames on the picked layout. The base's world in them goes to the director as options, since it's a world direction. One frame's tokens then hold for every screen.
  3. **The build** as one piece: the HUD to the tokens (Faber, Modulo), and the base to the frame (Tessera's meshes, Faber's layout).
- **Held until the frame is picked:** new visible UI pieces and new base dressing. Installs already passed (Pip), bug fixes and the director's removals go on.
