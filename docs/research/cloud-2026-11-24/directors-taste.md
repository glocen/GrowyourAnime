## The verdict, in five lines

1. **The director judges with the eye and the ear, against shipped games of the genre.** A yes to a written restatement is weak evidence: the Mask Ledger, the stat board and the base's size were each approved in words and rejected at sight. OBSERVED, section 4.
2. **Three tests explain most rejections:** is it anime as the genre's hits make it; is it mature and composed (never childish, corny, cheap, flat or empty); does the festival stay inside Lantern Row. OBSERVED, section 2.
3. **Our verdict asks none of the three.** It asks "Felt, Tier, the one bold thing", read at 64 px against our own feel line. That's how "competent" passed Watt, Rook's takes, the Ledger and four rounds of sound. OPINION, from section 4.
4. **Research is trusted on order and scope, much less on looks.** 8 rows record the director taking the option marked "(Recommended)": six on order, scope or structure, two on a look (Lantern Row's merge, 11-12; the Tower room kept, 11-23). On four other three-way look picks (AR11, AR18, AR24, AR29) the director chose differently from research each time. MEASURED.
5. **At least 11 misreadings are on record,** nearly all from widening a remark or silently choosing one reading of an unpunctuated sentence. Three cheap tools at the end fix most of it: a reference beside every take, a two-way restatement, ten questions.

## 7. Tool A: would the director pass this? (answer from a phone capture)

Every question is written so that "yes" passes. One "no" means don't show it yet. Seeds, to be corrected by their answers in section 8.

- **A Voidkin:** (1) Beside two reference units at the same height, is it the same make? (2) Can you name its archetype in three words, and is it a character, not a job? (3) Is it finished, with no rough mesh, facets or slab face? (4) Does its face differ from every other Voidkin's? (5) Is its detail right for its rarity?
- **A clip** (five seconds of video): (1) No tic repeating each loop? (2) Does the camera stay where the player put it? (3) Does a strike have a wind-up and a hit you can point at?
- **An effect:** (1) Is the main shape 3D or particles, not a flat drawn picture? (2) No outlined cartoon flame? (3) No blood, no wound (rule 4)?
- **A screen or HUD piece:** (1) At 3x zoom: no seam, halo, misaligned badge or colour break? (2) Does it sit on the dark glass they called good, not replace it? (3) Is every coloured word the colour of its meaning? (4) Is the chrome plainer than the art it frames? (5) Is every panel filled, none mostly empty? (6) Over a real fight, can you still see the fight? (7) Would it look out of place in a young child's game? If it wouldn't, it's "childish".
- **Text:** (1) Is each term the genre's own, or theirs as typed? (2) No line that narrates? (3) American spelling?
- **The world:** (1) No lantern, mask or stall outside Lantern Row? (2) By day, is it lighter than "somber"? (3) Does each machine read as what it does? (4) From the gate, is the plot filled?
- **Sound** (not from a capture): has the director's ear picked it on the audition panel? If not, it isn't done.

## 9. Tool C: how to read their messages

Their writing is fast, unpunctuated and sometimes carries words from another language (OPINION; the repo doesn't say which). Known forms: "i do not look like" means "I don't like"; "blend" means bland; "decal" means an icon's picture; "spartiate" means Spartan; "exemple"; "lightning" for lighting; "actual" may mean current.

**Five real sentences that read two ways:**

1. "i do not like the play button when we click on it it show a panels of gamemode for now it only show FOB" (11-19). **A:** I dislike that it shows a panel holding one mode. **B:** I dislike the button as it is; it should show a panel of modes. We took A. It was B.
2. "The whole main hud of the player need a refont dont u think?" (11-15). **A:** a new typeface. **B:** an overhaul; the same sentence ends "need a whole re design". Recorded as A. Not settled: question 10.
3. "But that realism is acc great" (11-15). **A:** keep it realistic, so no toon outlines. **B:** I like what's there, build on it. We took A. It was B.
4. "those 2d flame effect is horrendous" (11-19). **A:** the critical's comet stroke. **B:** the flame sprites round a fighter. Taken as A, unconfirmed.
5. "let keep red.. it js need a different type of color different width (smaller imo)" (11-15). **A:** health stays red, in another red. **B:** health stays red; the card takes another colour. Recorded as A. Not settled.

**Six habits:**

1. If a sentence holds "those", "this" or "it" with two candidates, or a dislike with no named object, send both readings as A and B, each with a crop.
2. "maybe", "imo" and "i think" mark a direction to try, never a spec. The register did this right on 11-19: "\"Maybe\" makes the gradients and motion a direction to try".
3. A list after "like", "such as" or "etc" is an example.
4. Their noun is the name. Never rename it.
5. A remark covers the thing on screen when it was said. Ask before carrying it elsewhere.
6. Restate a look with a picture; restate a rule with one worked number.
