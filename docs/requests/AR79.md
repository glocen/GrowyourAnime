# AR79. The base, overhauled whole: its concept, its look, and the Strip around it (research with Gemini; for the director's pick, then Modulo and Faber)

**The director, 2026-11-28, by 11:32:** "like base upgrade right now the whole concept is horrendous the ui etc need an overhaul" (`docs/decisions.md`, "Worlds and the base").

## Why it fails today (research, from the game's captures)

- **The place:** a 128-stud beige slab in an empty sky, behind a waist-high fence. It reads as a car park.
- **The look:** grey machines spread across it like props, raw trusses, and name boards on sticks that float over the fights.
- **The loop:** the Power screen has one right answer, Auto, and launch bays make 1.8 to 7.7 times what the machines draw. Voltage is never offered, and the battery's overflow comes back as "+31 Credits" (`docs/balance.md`, F3).
  - The directive warned against exactly this: "Do not invent a complicated electrical engineering simulator unless it demonstrably improves the game" (section on electricity, line 231).
- **The flex:** a passer-by sees a fence and a small plinth. Nothing at the base shows a Voidkin growing (TR76's spine).
- **The hub:** the Strip runs about 720 studs, 45 s end to end.

## Three directions, side by side, for the director's pick (2026-11-28, 11:40)

They're truly different from each other, with no recommendation (R4). In all three:
- the power slider goes, and electricity becomes one or two plain choices, with kWh spent on things a player wants (instant breaks, the shop's Power Deal, a boost);
- TR76's upgrades land at the base as a moment you can see;
- the plot shrinks to about 80 to 96 studs and the Strip tightens, so the shop is 6 to 7 s from any plot;
- the stick boards and fences go.

**1. The Void Dojo: a stepped fighters' compound.** About 96 by 104 studs.
- **What it is:** three terraces rising to a central Arc-Core over the Breaker, with worker pylons beaming power into it.
- **The 30 s beat:** your lead spars a training dummy in the front court with its real signature. At the Ascension Anvil, each Mastery, Star or Signature step sends an elemental shockwave across the compound.
- **Electricity:** one switch, Breach (faster breaks) or Drill (faster training), plus kWh to spend.
- **The levels:** each raises a terrace and the spire, with a Mastery crest above the roof at level 3.
- **What it keeps and costs:** it keeps every machine of the directive, folded into a few big pieces. About 52 to 70 h in all.

**2. The Sky-Keep: a base that grows upward.** About 80 by 92 studs.
- **What it is:** an open-front anime keep, one storey at level 1, two at level 2, three at level 3. Everything you touch is on the ground floor.
- **The upper floors:** worker balconies over the street, a training balcony, and a roof deck showing your Colosseum team. At level 3, an 18-stud hologram of your lead stands above the roof.
- **Electricity:** the façade's three-ring gauge runs on its own (Overclocked when the bays are well staffed), and kWh is spent in 20 kWh steps.
- **The flex:** a player's level reads from 300 studs away.
- **The director's call:** it moves the Tower's door onto the keep. About 62 to 80 h.

**3. The Rift Caldera: a sunken void crater with a floating Breaker.** About 96 by 96 studs.
- **What it is:** no fence, bridges over a glowing pit, and the Breaker as rings floating above it, with the queued shards circling it where passers-by see them.
- **The workers:** you seat your Voidkin on Breaker vents (faster breaks and kWh) or Catalyst vents (stones, jewels, training). Stones and jewels grow as crystals on the rim, and you sweep them up as you walk by.
- **The director's call:** it drops the Condenser, the Forge and the Training machine as machines of their own. About 60 to 78 h.

**The Strip, for any of them** (Gemini's, research's read):
- an octagonal plaza at the crossing, with the Void Shop and its big live clock on the south side and the Festival arch on the north;
- the Colosseum closing the view east, and the Calamity's sealed terrace west;
- the Towers' record obelisks on one raised ridge behind the plots;
- empty plots as clean pads;
- pale stone, dark curbs and brass, with distant floating monoliths on the horizon so the hub stops reading as a tabletop.

## The pick: the Void Dojo, with the Caldera's floating Breaker (research, 2026-11-28, 12:22, on the director's "your call")

- **Why the Dojo:**
  - it keeps every machine the directive and the director's words named, folded into a few big pieces;
  - it costs the least, about 52 to 70 h;
  - it puts the lead's growth in the front court, where it's played, not shown on a board.
- **Taken from the Caldera:** the Arc-Core is the Breaker as floating rings, with the queued shards circling it. That's one hero shape with motion, seen from the Strip.
- **Left out:**
  - the Sky-Keep's storeys: multi-floor rooms cost the most and are hard on a phone camera;
  - the Caldera's pit: bridges over a drop are risky to walk and to frame.
- **The design:** `docs/design.md`, "The base".
