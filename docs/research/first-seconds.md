# The first seconds of four games

Asked by the director, 2026-11-09: "looks at why grow a garden, grow a chicken fighter, steal an egg, throw food for an animal are addictive the first seconds". Read on 2026-11-09 by a research subagent, from the pages linked at the end. Everything here is SOURCED: none of it was tested by us, and no one on the team played these games for it. Guide sites carry some of the claims alone, and those are marked weak. Numbers of players are from Roblox's games API on the day.

These are patterns to learn from. Nothing of their look, names, screens or numbers is ours to take (`AGENTS.md`, rule 3).

## What the four share in the first 20 seconds

1. **You spawn on your own plot, holding the starter item, with no text tutorial.** Grow a Garden: two dirt patches, a shovel, 20 of its coins [1]. Throw Food For Animals: a carrot already in hand [26]. Grow a Garden's maker says Roblox leaves "no time for tutorials" [5].
2. **The first verb is one input, and it's the title:** plant, press a key on an egg, tap a throw meter, hatch [1][17][27][12].
3. **The first payout comes in seconds, as a multiple.** Grow a Garden: a carrot grows in about 7.6 seconds, and 20 coins of seeds sell for 62 [1]. The other three: timing not found.
4. **Income turns passive at once.** Pets and animals pay every second [24][26]. Two of the four advertise progress while you're away on their own pages [O1].
5. **A rarity ladder shows before the first purchase:** a shop grouped from Common upward [9], food from Common to Cosmic [26], a rarity rolled on the tutorial's hatch [12].
6. **A variable roll rides on the basic action:** Gold at 1% and Rainbow at 0.1% on each crop [4]; Gold at 13% and Big at 15% on each throw [29].
7. **Tiny servers put strangers' progress beside you:** at most 4, 8, 7 and 5 players [O1].
8. **Robux is on the first screen and never blocks:** a Robux price beside each seed, a paid restock, a pass [1].

## Why players leave early

- Roblox's recommendations penalise a game's "first-play bounce rate", measured under 60 seconds and from 61 to 180 [O2].
- Roblox's own onboarding page: players decide "within minutes"; teach the essentials, reach the fun fast, end on a moment of joy [O3].
- No first-session data was found for any of the four. One site's "40 to 60% lost before 300 seconds" has no dataset behind it (weak) [32].

## Rotating shops

- Grow a Garden: seeds and gear restock every 5 minutes, eggs every 30, the same stock on every server [3], with a countdown in the shop's header [1], a paid restock [1][9], and an official stock channel on Discord [3].
- The other three have no rotating shop. Steal An Egg's world respawns its eggs every 5 minutes, which may play the same part (our reading).
- **Whether a short timer holds players isn't measured anywhere we found.** It's testimony: "always something pulling you back" [2]. Grow a Garden fell from 21.6 million at once to about 13,000 with the timer unchanged [10][O1], so the timer alone doesn't hold a game (our reading).

## Each game, briefly

**Grow a Garden** (2025-03-25): buy seeds, plant, wait, sell, buy rarer seeds. Grows while you're away [O1]. A weekly update, every Saturday [5]. Mutations come from the weather and stack: Gold 20 times, Rainbow 50 [4]. Crops can be stolen for Robux [8]. Peaks: 21.6 million at once in June 2025 [10], 22.3 million on 2025-08-23 [8]. On 2026-11-09: 36.0 billion visits, 13,090 playing [O1]. Its maker credits growth while away, "the persistent world" and joy without competition [5][6].

**Grow a Chicken Fighter** (2026-06-16): hatch, feed, fight in an arena for money, fuse, climb a tower, rebirth [12][14][16]. Its tutorial is the first hatch, the first fuse and the first fight [12]. Rarity from Common to Mythic, fixed at the hatch; a fusion makes a mutated fighter [12]. Servers of 8 [O1]. No rotating shop found. On 2026-11-09: 123.9 million visits, 1,232 playing [O1]; trackers disagree on its peak (242,000 or 94,600) [15][16]; average session 26.1 minutes on one tracker [15]. No statement from its makers found.

**Steal An Egg** (2026-07-25): take an egg from a shared forest, run it home while a guardian chases, hatch it, and pets pay per second; train speed to reach farther [17][18]. Players raid each other [17]. Servers of 7 [O1]. Peak 14.27 million on one tracker [22]; on 2026-11-09: 10.88 billion visits, 522,101 playing [O1]. It was pulled in late August 2026: Roblox restricted feeds that pay players to keep watching, for its Kids and Select programs, on 2026-08-25 [O4][19]. It came back without the feed.

**Throw Food For Animals** (2026-09-18): throw food on a timing meter, an animal comes, and placed in your base it pays per second [26][28]. Five mutations and nine sizes, rolled on each throw [29]. Servers of 5 [O1]. No stealing or trading found. On 2026-11-09: 138.0 million visits, 136,666 playing [O1].
