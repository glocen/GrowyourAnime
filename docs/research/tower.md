# The tower in the director's reference

Asked by the director, 2026-11-09: "a tower system like grow a chicken fighter on each player base behind there is a tower rush (look how it work)". Read on 2026-11-09 by a research subagent, from guides, the game's page, its pass list and patch notes relayed from its Discord. Everything here is SOURCED, and guide sites that stand alone are marked weak. No video could be read, and nobody on the team played it for this, so what the director saw in the game outranks any line below. We take how it works. Nothing of its names, art or numbers is ours to use (`AGENTS.md`, rule 3).

## The finding

Its Tower is a solo climb: one fighter, floor by floor, against the game's own opponents, whose level rises with the floor. It pays cash on each floor and it gates the rebirth. **No sign that a floor holds another player's team,** and no mode named "tower rush" was found. Fighting the teams of players who aren't online is in a different mode there, the Arena, and only by inference.

So the director's idea, a Tower whose floors hold other players' fighters, isn't in the reference. It's ours.

## How its Tower works

- **Where:** not confirmed. One guide puts it "in the central arena" [6], and a button on the screen sends the fighter there [4][9]. Nothing found says it stands on each plot.
- **Opponents:** "Climb the TOWER and beat every boss" (official [3]). A recorded run shows the game's own creatures with levels, one a floor: level 104 on floors 89 and 90 [4]. No player's name appears anywhere (so written opponents: inferred).
- **A run:** one fighter [7][13]. It fights by itself, and the player helps with three buttons: one that cheers, one that stands by, one that retreats [4]. Health doesn't come back between floors (weak [13]). A knockout ends the run, "locks in a record" and pays out, then asks whether to keep climbing or stop [4]. Leaving or losing returns to the bottom (weak [13]).
- **How high:** no cap found. Reported bests are 90 [4] and just over 100 (weak [21]).
- **What it pays:** cash on each clear, rising with the floor and with the rebirth count (weak [13][15]). The rebirth opens at a floor shown in its panel, higher each time: 10 for the first, 31 by the sixth [7].
- **Limits:** no ticket, energy, cooldown, daily reset or season found.
- **Skips:** a lift rides to a floor already reached, for a fee (weak [10][13]).
- **A board** of best floors is reported (weak [13]).
- **What players say:** "the clearest measure of a fighter's ceiling" [4]. One review calls the loop clean and says the game "needs more breadth" [19]. A wall is reported near floor 20 (weak [20]).

## Its other two places to fight

- **The shared pit,** in the middle: live, against the fighters of the players present, on servers of 8 [1][14]. Events land there every 7 minutes for 3, with fighting between players off during a boss [9]. Winning streaks raise the pay [5].
- **The Arena,** added 2026-08-22 and opened late in the game: from a menu, three against "another player's three", one minute, with a look at the opponent and a skip before the fight, abilities by hand or automatic, trophies, monthly seasons and a board of 100 [12][16][17][18]. Live or saved: stated nowhere. The look, the skip and a fix against idle farming point to saved teams (inferred).

## What it sells (official [2])

Four passes, none random: the lift at half price, 149 Robux; every fight pays double, 299; an automatic rebirth, 999; a bundle, 1,499.
