# Risks

What could sink the game or cost weeks, and the cheapest proof that retires each. Research's register, rewritten 2026-11-11 for the design as it stands. A risk leaves when its proof is in, with the date.

| # | The risk | Why it's real here | The cheapest proof | Who, when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The loop isn't worth playing without art.** | an automatic lead can feel like watching | TR2: the circuit in grey, judged in motion | Faber, research; phase 1 |
| 2 | **Thirty-one Voidkin cost more than the team has.** | each needs a design, a model, clips and a signature at the bar, from one maker; five need a custom rig | AR1 and AR4 timed; the count at launch is what's finished | Modulo; phase 1 to 2 |
| 3 | **R6 Voidkin look poor, or the clips read badly.** | the studio has never made an R6 clip reel | AR1's reel, judged before a second Voidkin starts | Modulo, research; phase 1 |
| 4 | **Balance runs away.** | deep stats from seven sources | the one rule in `docs/balance.md`, a failing test when a source multiplies, the simulation rerun on the game's own rows | Faber, research; from TR2 |
| 5 | **The numbers are wrong in play.** | every value is a seed; the pace table is a guess | numbers exposed at runtime; the first real sessions measured against the pace table | research; phase 2 on |
| 6 | **A Voidkin or an item is lost or doubled.** | they're the valuables, and breaking, forging and buying each spend one thing to make another | one function per change, operation ids, each transaction repeated and interrupted in a logic case | Faber, then QA; TR2 |
| 7 | **The save library doesn't fit.** | the director's data system was shaped for another game; here a profile holds many owned things | TR1's call, with a profile of 31 Voidkin and 200 items saved and loaded | Faber; TR1 |
| 8 | **Time away is cheated or wrong.** | the Breaker, the bays and the battery run on the clock while the player is gone | server stamps only; pure-rule cases over time; a real leave and return | Faber; TR2 |
| 9 | **Gear as the account's turns out wrong.** | it's research's largest change to the package | the director's second look before phase 3; nothing of gear is built before then | the director, research |
| 10 | **Lifesteal breaks boss fights.** | it heals by damage dealt, and bosses have far more health than a lead | its per-second ceiling; watched first in play | research; phase 2 |
| 11 | **A phone can't hold eight busy bases and an arena.** | workers, machines, champions and effects in one view | a stand-in crowd measured on a mid phone before the Strip is dressed | Faber; phase 2 |
| 12 | **The Clash is farmed.** | a second account feeding wins | rewards scaled by strength, a wait per victim, a daily cap per pair; QA tries to break it | research, QA; phase 5 |
| 13 | **A sale trips Roblox's paid random items rule.** | removal and a mark on the account (`docs/roblox-rules.md`) | nothing random sold at launch; the store is its own approved piece | the director, research; phase 6 |
| 14 | **A design is too close to a published character.** | a valid claim takes the game down | the content check on every sheet, before any model | Modulo, the check; every sheet |
| 15 | **Uploads don't play in the group's game.** | Roblox plays only what the experience's owner holds | the first clip and the first sound uploaded from the game's own place and played there | Faber; with AR1 |
| 16 | **The game grows faster than it's proven.** | six grounds, a Tower, an arena and a world boss | phases end on something playable; the cut line; one ground at the bar first | everyone |
| 17 | **One PC.** | 15.5 GB of memory, two Studios at most, one render at a time | seats open only when work waits; turns on the board | the director |
| 18 | **The heaviest players run out.** | the simulation has them through six grounds in about two weeks | the Tower, the Clash, stars and the Mythical hunt; an update planned before launch | research; phase 4 |
