  - Our own renders and captures of the game, posed and lit in Blender or the game, finished as image art (rule 2).

  - Rule 3: no shipped icon's composition, frame or type; the shipped icons are for size and read only.

### Research: the three rejected, and the top games' icons studied (2026-11-27, 17:15)

- **The director, by 17:10:** "Imfg what is this game page icon this is horrendous plain terribly bad do research ln tops game page browse roblox", then "Why rook stop making stuff then making it the center of the world". A, B and C are rejected, and Rook isn't the icon's centre (`docs/decisions.md`, Release).
- **The study (OBSERVED):** 18 top games' icons at 512, from Roblox's public thumbnails (`build/reference/game-icons/`, internal and gitignored; the sheet is `_sheet.png`). Eleven are anime games: Anime Vanguards, Anime Adventures, Anime Defenders, Anime Last Stand, Anime Rangers X, Arise Crossover, Blox Fruits, King Legacy, Jujutsu Infinite, Sailor Piece and The Strongest Battlegrounds.
  1. **Painted, not rendered.** Every anime icon is a painted 2D anime illustration. None shows the game's own avatar as a 3D render. Ours were renders of the in-game make, and beside them they read as a plastic toy.
  2. **The face is the subject.** The eyes and expression fill a third to half of the frame, and the look is intense: a glare, a grin, glowing eyes. The crop is tight, with the figure running off the edges.
  3. **Energy everywhere.** Saturated and high contrast: lightning, aura and sparks, depth in layers, a rim light.
  4. **A crafted logotype, never a banner.** Seven carry a bevelled, glowing title with a mark of its own, and four carry no text. None has a plain strip of words like our "Early Access" bar.
  5. Often two figures, or a figure and a creature.
- **The cause (R5):** the technique, not the finish: a render of the in-game make on a flat gradient, under a banner. It changes.
- **What the bar takes:** a painted anime illustration and a logotype. Rule 2 has us make every asset, and AI-made images are out. Our own tools haven't reached painted illustration: painted UI read as "a sketch" (2026-11-27, 02:04).
- **The options, for the director (R4, no recommendation):**
  - **(a) An illustrator** paints the icon and the logotype, and later the thumbnails. It costs money, so it's the director's call (rule 1), as for Home's cards.
  - **(b) Our own make at its best:** a Blender render finished in an anime style (toon light, ink lines, painted effects). It's face-first, with a crafted logotype, and Rook isn't the centre. By the study, a render reads below the painted bar.
  - **(c) Art the director brings.**
- **"Early Access"** stays in the title (the director, 2026-11-17: "in the title and on the page"). The title shows on the page, so the icon needn't carry it.
- **Status:** the targets stop here. The thumbnails wait on the icon's direction.
