# AR70. The game page's icon and thumbnails (Modulo's targets, the director's pick)

**Research, 2026-11-27, 14:08.** Tier 2: research and Modulo. Nothing yet covers the game page's art, and the early access needs it on the game page (`docs/production-plan.md`, phase 6). Publishing stays the director's.

- **Feel line:** a player scrolling Roblox's Home sees our icon at its small size among the anime games, and stops on it. One Voidkin mid-strike, in the game's own light, the dark slate and the ember of the HUD, and nothing else competing.
- **What it contains:**
  - **The icon:** square, 512 × 512. That size is sourced from the developer forum only (Roblox's docs don't state it); check it at the upload.
  - **Three thumbnails:** 16:9, 1920 × 1080, each under 3 MB (official: [Roblox's thumbnails page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)). A fight in the festival grounds, a shard's reveal, and the bays at work. Each shows only what is in the game today.
  - **"Early Access"** on the art where it reads (the director, 2026-11-17, by 12:16).
- **What waits:** a video thumbnail, the store's art, and event art.
- **The make:**
  - Our own renders and captures of the game, posed and lit in Blender or the game, finished as image art (rule 2).
  - Rook leads, since his make is the most finished. Kirra joins only after the director's look at her.
  - Rule 3: no shipped icon's composition, frame or type; the shipped icons are for size and read only.
  - Rule 4: nothing misleading, no promise of Robux or of anything not in the game. The content check runs before any upload.
- **Targets first (R4, a new direction):** two or three icon options at final quality, each shown at 512 px and at Home's small size, beside shipped anime games' icons at the same size. The options go to the director side by side, with no recommendation from research. The thumbnails follow the picked icon's look. Cap: 3 hours for the targets.
- **Four facts:** asked; not made; not tested; not accepted.
