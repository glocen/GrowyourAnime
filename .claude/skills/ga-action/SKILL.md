# One action, built as one thing (shared by the team)

Its speed, range, tell, release, hit, animation, effect, sound, camera and recovery make sense together or not at all. This page says the order only. The asset pipeline, `/ga-studio-test` and the content check apply unchanged.

The **replay** below is a debug command that replays the action from the player's camera on a clean test pad, with the numbers live: `NL_Debug:Invoke("Replay", who, action, every?)` (`src/server/Services/Main/ReplayService.luau`).

1. **The feel line** (research with the director; from the benchmark moment and the director's recorded words while the director is away). One sentence: who does what and how it feels, from the player's camera. Under it: the benchmark moment with its timestamp and its numbers; what's ours; the boundaries (rules 2 to 4, the director's calls on this piece); one hero shape, named. The test: after ten presses on the dummy, do you want an eleventh?
   - **The benchmark, if this game has one** (none for this game: the feel line and the hero shape are the target). The director names it per game, and with none, the feel line and the hero shape are the whole target. For every action, clip and effect it has, its moment is the target on seven measures: timing, size against the body, screen share at its peak, life in frames, read at 64 px, kind of motion (a sheet laid and flown, a smear that trails, a snap) and intensity. Research measures them once from the footage. The piece is judged beside it in motion, at the same rate, from the same view.
   - **The system behind the moment.** Matching a moment isn't enough. Work out the rule that produces it, build that, and test it on the cases the footage never shows: a hit mid-action, a cancel, in the air, at a wall, a press during a blend. What's inferred from footage is labelled DESIGN, never OBSERVED.
   - **What stays ours:** the shapes, the textures, the poses, the names, the sound and the colours. Nothing is traced, matched pose for pose or made from a frame. Feel means timing, weight and read, never the whole look. The director's calls on a piece beat its moment.
   - **A piece the benchmark doesn't have** takes the nearest moment for timing and intensity, then the feel line's hero shape.
   - **Only research finds references.** It gives the makers the moment and the numbers, and their craft reading of it stays theirs.
   - **An effect's technique, named per part:** a mesh, a Beam or Trail, particles, a light, or a card. A card only takes a thin strand or a flat moment, never a shape with a near and a far side (`handbook/visuals/vfx.md`, "Technique by kind"; `docs/asset-requests.md`, AR8, "The method"). Research checks the technique here, before any block-in.
   - **Scope is fixed here.** Before a piece's first commit, its entry names what the block contains and what waits for the shared finish. Nothing joins a piece after its feel line: a fix outside it is its own block or a line on `docs/polish-later.md`. A signature piece or a hard technical problem is named as such here, and gets the time it needs.
2. **The beats.** Named beats (Tell, Wind, Release, Contact, Recover, or the action's own names) in the action's Definition or Config entry, feel numbers first (startup, active, recovery, cancel-from, movement during, knockback, hitstop frames), geometry second. The clip's markers carry the beat names. The effect, the hit, the sound and the camera hang on the same names, and a self-test asserts that markers and beats match. Research measures the benchmark moment once, in under an hour, into the spec's own section. Research and the asset maker sign the Definition's line, dated: that's the design approval (tier 2).
3. **The blockout** (the asset maker and the builder together, hours, the same day): key poses on the control rig, a stand-in effect (the effects kit in grey, played from its rows), the real timing, in the replay. Variants when the idea is uncertain, one when it's clear. Research's verdict in `/ga-review`'s form; the director's pick when present. The build starts from the chosen blockout. A new idea starts a new blockout, never a patch. Trying an idea here is always tier 1.
4. **The build,** to the beats: the asset maker makes the clip, and the look of a signature effect (shape, texture, life, in its preview beside the clip). The builder makes the effect's code, the hit, the sound (one short library cue, logged in `docs/asset-log.md`) and the camera. The numbers are tried by playing through `NL_Debug:Invoke("Tune", path, value)`, never by edit-and-restart. A self-test joins it all on the beat names. Never a clip reviewed without its effect, or an effect without its clip.
5. **The close:** QA's one pack from the replay, beside the benchmark at the same rate and from the same view; research's pass, and `ga-fresh-eyes` blind on a signature piece; the content check; `/ga-finish`. Two fix rounds on one idea, then back to step 3 with the options for the director.

**The director's two checkpoints:** the blockout and the final. A remark made mid-build is a note for the next checkpoint, not a rule. Research collects it under the piece's entry, and nobody rewrites a spec, a feel line or a Config on it until that checkpoint, unless the director says "change it now". A teammate who hears one sends it to research as said and carries on. If a remark contradicts the feel line, research asks once, at the checkpoint, and records one answer.
