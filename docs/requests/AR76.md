## Research: the targets, none picked; the fire's technique changes (2026-11-28, 02:50)

### Round 2's technique

- **The fire's material:** simulated fire and smoke, rendered to our own 8x8 flipbook sheets. Blender's Mantaflow, offline, under `build/locks/blender.lock` and the memory guard: a fire body, a dark smoke body, and a white-hot core. In the build they play on ParticleEmitters and mesh flipbooks (vfx-reference, "From the vault", items 7 and 11).
- **Dark values in every frame:** smoke wraps the fire, debris kicks up, and the burn stays on the floor as a scorch.
- **The hold:**
  - the mass sits low, about a body's height over the foe, never at the frame's edge;
  - it gathers: embers and heat pulled in from frame 20, growing to 52, never shrinking before the drop.
  - B5's composition stays the director's (the mass over the foe, down with the fist, the foe pinned).
- **The strike:** the slam's size lands on 57, with a smear from the fist down, and the knuckles lit white and visible at the peak.
- **The peak:** at least twice the figures' width, low and wide along the floor, with nothing upright round the foe (no stake, no brazier).
- **Targets:** two again, after AR77's targets: the same sheet with one take in the place's own light, never day and night (the director, 2026-11-28, 02:5x).
