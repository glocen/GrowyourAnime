# The long grind of the director's reference

Asked by the director, 2026-11-09: "we should add a lot of upgrade features and luck ... take some inspiration from sailor piece a complete differents game but their endless grinding is incredible". Read on 2026-11-09 by a research subagent. Everything here is SOURCED, through a tool that summarises pages: check a number at its link before quoting it. Guide sites that stand alone are marked weak. We take the structure. Nothing of its names, art or numbers is ours to use (`AGENTS.md`, rule 3).

## The finding

Its grind is about fifteen separate upgrade tracks on one character. Seven are rerolls, each with its own token, its own rarity ladder and usually a counter that guarantees the top result. The tokens are also sold for Robux.

**It didn't last.** 745,280 players at once on 2026-04-19, the day after a large update [3][4][5]. 1,046 on 2026-11-09 [1]. The grind held while updates landed almost every week: six in the seven weeks to late April, one in June, then a long gap [5][8][14].

## The game

Made 2025-11-17 by a Roblox group. 1.60 billion visits. Servers of 12. A level cap of 25,000 [1]. Average session 21.1 minutes on one tracker [3]. Retention: not found.

**Earned:**

- **Ascension:** ten tiers, each a checklist across the whole game (level, kills, boss kills, damage, dungeon clears, rare boss items). Each pays more money, more soft currency and more luck. What it resets: not found [18].
- **Mastery** per weapon, by kills, capped [19]. **Enchant** on accessories: 70% to succeed, and a failure keeps the level and adds 10% to the next try [20]. **Artifacts** in sets with rolled stats, and a 40-level collection track that pays reroll tokens [21].
- **On the avatar:** auras from random crates, with a damage bonus [23]. Titles from kill counts and rare boss drops [7][25].

## Bosses

- Island bosses return about every 5 minutes. World bosses share a 30-minute timer for the server. Summoned bosses cost a key that other bosses drop [7][24].
- **Sharing:** deal 10% of the boss's full health or receive nothing. Guides tell players to use private servers [7][24].
- **Drops:** reroll tokens at 50 to 100%, chase items at 0.75 to 3%. Every 25th kill guarantees a rare one, and world bosses carry their own counter of 70 to 155 kills [7][24].

## Luck

- One luck value, fed by about twelve systems, from 100% to roughly 700%. A Robux product and events each double it [11][25]. It raises drop rates. Its effect on reroll odds: not found.
- 127 developer products and no passes: token packs, crates, boosts, direct unlocks [11]. Whether odds are shown before a purchase: not found.

## What players say

- **For:** fast early levels, frequent updates, generous codes, a curve that keeps rising, playing in a party [6].
- **Against:** repetitive farming, power gated by luck, bots flooding the economy with rare items, a thin update easiest to unlock with Robux (weak) [8][29].

## Research's reading (inferred)

1. **Many tracks side by side,** one token each, whose bonuses multiply. A cheaper track is always left to improve.
2. **Every action can drop some track's token.** No minute is wasted.
3. **Fixed counters** turn bad luck into a goal with an end.
4. **Luck is a stat** that every track feeds, so each upgrade speeds the next.
5. **Tracks open at spaced gates, and a checklist pushes the player through every mode.**

And two warnings: the grind needed a weekly update to hold, and its income came from selling rerolls, which is the part Roblox's rules now bind (`docs/roblox-rules.md`, "Paid random items").
