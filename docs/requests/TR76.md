# TR76. The game's shape for launch: the spine, the Lantern District, the Colosseum, the Void Shop, the Index, the Tower (research with Gemini; for the director's yes, then the team)

**The director, 2026-11-28, by 11:10** (word log): Gemini helps craft the game. The loop's spine is the rewards that upgrade Voidkin. An Index, a shop UI, the Void Shop reworked, a Colosseum designed by research, more systems at research's judgment, the Tower's big upgrade. The teammates finish their current work first.

**Source:** Gemini's design consult, `build/scratch/quaestor/gemini/transcripts/2026-11-28_game-design.md` (UNVERIFIED until research checks each number against `docs/balance.md`). Research's own calls are marked.

**The restatement, for the director's yes (tier 3: scope and economy). Nothing goes into design.md before it.**
1. **The spine: each Voidkin grows itself.** Mastery ranks 1 to 5 from fighting with it, and Signature levels 1 to 5 from Skill Manuals. Stars get perks at 1, 3, 5 and 7. About 6% of a build moves from gear onto the fighter, additive. Why: gear holds 58.5% of a level-60 build and a Voidkin's Stars 3.5% (Gemini, from balance.md), so upgrading a Voidkin barely shows.
2. **The Festival of Battles becomes the Lantern District** (research's call under the 11:05 grant, from Gemini's ranking of TR75; its look goes to the director as options):
   - an open district shared by the server, with four wings each paying one kind of upgrade (XP and Credits, Forge stones, jewels, emblem cases) and camps back in 10 to 18 s by head count;
   - three camps fill your Flame, which opens your own Lamplighter at the Belfry (Normal or Hard, chosen at the gate);
   - every camp anyone clears fills the server's brazier, and at full a 90 s Festival Surge brings a shared Envoy to the plaza.
   - It replaces the run, the stretches, the checkpoints and the Keeper alone.
3. **The Void Colosseum** on the main map, in place of the Clash Grounds:
   - ranked duels against other players' saved builds (one lead, or a relay of three), 5 rewarded bouts a day, Arena Seals, and weekly tiers;
   - live spars against anyone in the server, in front of the stands.
4. **The Void Shop, reworked:** six slots that restock every 5 min, the same worldwide. One slot is priced in kWh, so electricity finally buys something. A rare top slot is called out to the server. A weekly Exchange tab sells fixed rewards for Seals and Tower Crests, including a Named Voidkin copy of your choice, two a week. No Robux anywhere in it.
5. **The Index:** Voidkin, Shades, gear designs, emblems and jewels. Each discovery pays, and the milestones add up to about 2.5% of a build.
6. **The Tower, upgraded:** start at your record minus 5. A Warden every fifth floor, with a heal and a sure chest. The room's sky changes every 10 floors. A weekly rule, and Tower Crests.
7. **Credits:** the shop gets about a quarter of early spending from hour 0 (today it gets none before 5 h).
- **Not in this yes, later candidates:** shiny cosmetic variants, a light Rift at the base, Equip Best, and inspecting a neighbour's showcase.
- **The order:** current pieces finish first. Then the rules in pure Luau and the UI kit; then the main map, the shop and the Index; then the Lantern District; then the Tower and the Colosseum.
