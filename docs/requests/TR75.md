## Research: three directions, for the director's pick (2026-11-28, 11:05)

A new direction goes to the director side by side, with no recommendation (R4). In all three:
- the waves, the Shades' roles, the elites' moves and the Lamplighter's three phases carry over (TR69);
- Normal and Hard stay;
- loot rolls per fall and is tuned to the hour.

**1. The Night Market: an open district, shared by the server.**
- **What it is:** Lantern Row is one open festival district, a loop of lantern streets round a central plaza, shared by everyone in the server. Walk in through the gate: no run, no start button.
- **The camps:** Shade troupes hold places round the loop, each with one idea: Brawlers in the alley, a Guard wall on the bridge, Dashers in the long lane, an elite in the courtyard. Clear one and its lanterns relight and it pays; it's retaken after about 45 s.
- **The boss:** every camp anyone clears fills the district's festival meter. When it's full, the Lamplighter descends on the plaza as a server boss. Everyone near fights, and everyone who hit gets paid.
- **Hard:** the inner district behind a gate that opens after your first Lamplighter.
- **What it changes:** "keepers are fought alone" (a director's decision) becomes a shared boss.

**2. The Lantern Trials: one arena, a night of rounds.**
- **What it is:** Lantern Row's heart is one great festival arena, ringed by stalls and crowds of lanterns. A night is six rounds in the same arena, each with its own rule: the Brawler troupe, the Guards' wall, the Dashers' lanes, the elite, a mix, then the Lamplighter. No walking between fights.
- **Between rounds:** 5 s to pick one of three festival charms (heal 30%, more damage this night, a faster signature), a decision every minute.
- **A fall:** the night ends and you keep what you earned. A night is about 4 to 5 min.
- **Hard:** ten rounds, with curses among the charms.
- **What it changes:** it sits nearest the Tower (a room, then the next foe). The rounds' rules and the charms set it apart.

**3. Void Rifts: a hub, and pockets of the Void to enter.**
- **What it is:** Lantern Row is a festival square, the hub. Void rifts tear open round it on a timer, each with its rank shown above it (E to S).
- **Entering a rift:** walk into one alone, or friends follow within 10 s. Inside is a pocket of the Void dressed as a piece of the festival: three short rooms of waves, then a guardian (an elite, or at the high ranks the Lamplighter). About 3 min.
- **The rank** sets the level and the loot, and replaces Normal and Hard.
- **What it changes:** it takes the parked Rift's place, and players choose their challenge each time.

**After the pick:** the map's layout as a top-down plan beside a shipped map, then the target paint-overs at the player's camera (Modulo), then the grey block in the Festival place (Faber), judged in play.
