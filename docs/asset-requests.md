# Asset requests

The makers' queue, in order. A piece's record is its entry here: the feel line, what it delivers, the checks, the verdict, the status. The look every fighter is held to: `docs/north-star.md`, "The bar". The line every fighter's design stays inside: `docs/decisions.md`, "Fighters".

## AR1. The first three fighters, on one sheet

For Modulo. Research's request, 2026-11-09. A design sheet in grey, not models: it sets who the game's people are before anything is built. Step 3 of `docs/production-plan.md` builds the first of them.

**Feel line:** three people you can tell apart as black shapes at 64 px, each caught mid-move, each someone you'd want to own.

**Delivers,** on one sheet, to `build/handoff/Fighters/`:

- three original fighters, one of each type: close, blade, ranged;
- for each: the silhouette in black; the R6 body in grey from the front and from three quarters; its stance; the key pose of one signature move; three colour chips; two name candidates; one line on who it is;
- the same sheet at 64 px, over a dark ground and a bright one, for the content check.

**The line** (`docs/decisions.md`, "Fighters"): each fighter starts from a type the whole genre shares, and its look, its move and its name are ours. Before the sheet goes to the check, ask of each: would a fan name a published character at a glance? If yes, change the design, not the name.

**Yours to settle:** how hair, outfits and weapons sit on R6 (meshes, textures, or both), with the cost of each in triangles and in your hours, noted here. It decides what a fighter costs to make.

**Checks:** the content check on the sheet; research's verdict here. No model before both.

**Status:** open. Starts Wednesday 2026-11-11, when the craft upgrade's effects half (NEXT Button's repo, AR29) is judged as it stands, unless the director says otherwise.
