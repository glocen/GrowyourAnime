# Risks

What could sink the game or cost weeks, and the cheapest proof that retires each one. Research's register, 2026-11-09. A risk leaves when its proof is in, with the result noted in the piece that gave it. Nothing here is measured yet.

| # | The risk | Why it's real here | The cheapest proof | Who, when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The first minute doesn't hold.** | Roblox counts a first play under 60 seconds against the game, and the two anime reskins of the chicken game sit at zero players (`docs/research/similar-games.md`) | TR2's grey loop, timed, with research's verdict. Then a few real first-time players before any art | Faber, research: step 1 |
| 2 | **A fighter is lost or doubled.** | fighters are the valuables. Fusing, releasing, buying and breaking all spend one thing to make another, and a request can arrive twice or stop halfway | every such change is one server function with an operation id. Logic cases for the repeat and for the stop between each pair of steps. A real leave during a break | Faber, QA: step 2 |
| 3 | **The save library doesn't fit.** | the director's data system was built for another game's shape. Here a profile holds many owned fighters, each with its own state | the builder's call in TR1, with the reason. A profile of 500 fighters saved, loaded and measured against the store's size limit | Faber: step 0 and step 2 |
| 4 | **Time away is cheated or wrong.** | Breakers, training and income run on the clock while the player is gone | all time is the server's stamps, never the client's. Cases for a rejoin loop, a long absence and a clock that goes backwards | Faber, QA: step 4 |
| 5 | **Fights can be forged, or differ between server and client.** | the client only plays back what the server settled | the rules are pure Luau run without Studio: the same teams and seed give the same list of events, every time | Faber: step 1 |
| 6 | **Balance runs away.** | ten rarities, levels, stars, traits and mutations multiply. The directive forbids uncontrolled growth (section 5) | a balance run over the game's own rows before any formula is built: a full-built Legendary against a plain Mythical, the time to each next purchase, the Grid's curve | research, with the builder's review: before step 4 |
| 7 | **R6 fighters cost too much to make, or look poor.** | every fighter needs hair, outfit and weapon on a six-part body, and its own clips | AR1's sheet settles how they're built. The first fighter is timed end to end at step 3, and the launch count is set from that | Modulo: step 3 |
| 8 | **New Roblox animation features leave R6 behind.** | the studio's craft upgrade aimed its clips at R15 (`docs/decisions.md`, "Challenged", 6) | the three facts of that upgrade's animation half, asked of R6 first | Modulo, Faber: before step 3 |
| 9 | **A phone can't hold eight busy bases.** | eight bases of animated fighters, lit cables, a shop's beam and eight Towers in one view | a stand-in crowd at the planned count on a real phone, before the kit is made. A budget per base from what it shows | Faber, the director's phone: step 5, and a first reading at step 1 |
| 10 | **The Tower's saved teams need a store shared between servers.** | every server reads and writes the same lists, and Roblox meters that | the builder's cost for it, on free services only (`AGENTS.md`, rule 1). Written teams stand in until then | Faber: before step 4 |
| 11 | **A sale trips the paid random items rule.** | for a game that doesn't comply: removed, and the account suspended (`docs/roblox-rules.md`) | nothing random is sold at launch. Before any product: the content check, and the two open questions in that file answered | research, the director: before any product |
| 12 | **A rights holder claims a fighter.** | a valid claim takes the game down and puts a warning on the director's account | the test in `docs/decisions.md`, "Fighters", on every sheet before a model is made | research, the content check: every sheet |
| 13 | **Uploads don't play in the group's game.** | Roblox only plays animations and private sounds that the experience's owner holds | the first clip and the first sound are uploaded to the group and played in the place before a second is made | Faber: step 3 |
| 14 | **The game grows faster than it's proven.** | day one added a Tower, Calamity, deep electricity and six tracks to the directive's loop | the plan's order holds: nothing enters without its blockout, and the slice's verdict picks what's next | research; the director |
| 15 | **One PC.** | 15.5 GB of memory, two Studios at most, one render at a time, and every open tab spends tokens | seats open only when work waits for them (`docs/production-plan.md`, "The team") | the director |
| 17 | **The spine makes waiting the best move.** | spare power is the income, so a base with nothing loaded earns most, and a big shard on a small Grid stops the credits | TR2's switch between the two economies, played both ways; pause on every machine | research: step 1 |
| 18 | **The grow has no roll at launch.** | a shard names its fighter, and the four reference games all put a roll on the basic action | a mutation roll on every break from TR2 on | Faber, research: step 1 |
| 19 | **The Tower is farmed or fished.** | a second account feeding gifts, a rejoin to change a floor's holder, a lift used over and over for credits, a server hop to refill the shop's limit | gifts wait and are capped; holders pinned; repeats pay no credits; purchases saved by rotation. A logic case for each | Faber, QA: step 4 |
| 20 | **One fighter number carries fights and income.** | a rarity step of a third gives about 13 times from bottom to top: too flat for prices, and steeper breaks the directive's section 5 | a balance run with the growth in stations and Grid upgrades | research: before step 4 |
| 16 | **The grind needs a weekly update to hold.** | the director's reference for long grinds lost its players when updates slowed (`docs/research/long-grind.md`) | content as rows from the start, proven by adding one new shard and its fighter's data without touching code | Faber: step 4 |
