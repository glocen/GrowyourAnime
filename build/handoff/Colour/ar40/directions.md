# AR40: three colour directions by day, as values

From `tools/colour/ar40_directions.py`; the values are in `directions.json`, beside this. `chips.png` is flat colour, top to bottom Today, A, B, C: a machine on the yard with the HUD's card, the bag, then the swatches (body, frame, fitting, steel, trim, the wall, the stones, the sun, the fill). It's no render.

- **Today:** void stone in dark violet, navy frames, a lilac fill, black glass
- **A, porcelain and gold:** the machines in warm white stone on slate frames with gold fittings; a warm white sun; the HUD and the bag as cream paper with slate ink. The brightest of the three, and the furthest from today.
- **B, sky steel:** the machines in light blue steel with white frames, the void's violet only in their cores; a clear blue fill under a warm white sun; the HUD and the bag in white with sky blue, gold for what's chosen.
- **C, sandstone and teal:** the machines in warm pale stone on teal bronze, the violet core against the sand; a gold day; the HUD and the bag stay a glass with pale words, lifted from black to a mid slate teal with gold. The nearest to today on screen, the warmest in the world.

A ground is one of ten large surfaces: the body's head and foot, the frame, two far shards, the stones, the day's fill, the HUD's panel, the bag's panel and its cover. Violet: a hue from 235 to 310 degrees with colour in it. Black: under L* 30. Words need 4.5 to 1 on their panel.

Today's stones are read by eye off one capture, an estimate. The stones' tint (a surface's Color only darkens, so a tint alone loses the light named): A `#F4FFC8`, which wants 1.46 times the light; B `#E7FFDD`, which wants 1.53 times the light; C `#FCFFBA`, which wants 1.51 times the light.
