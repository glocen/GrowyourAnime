# AR77. The fall, overhauled (research's call; Scintilla, then Faber)

## The director's word (2026-11-28, 02:34)

"that explosion effect when any enemy or voidkin or anything die is horrible."

This covers the fall that every body plays when it goes down: AR69's ShadeFall, the ball of fire at the foot (`Config.EffectRows/ShadeFall.json`, `Effects.ShadeFall`). AR69's burn (StateBurn) isn't covered and stays. The keeper's lantern fall (`Config.Effects.Falls`) is a separate effect and isn't named, so it stays as it is.

## Research: the call

- **Overhauled, not iterated.** AR69 has had two rounds on the fireball. The idea itself is wrong: fire has nothing to do with the Void, and the Shades are now slate-teal Void matter with lit cracks (AR75).
- **AR69's round 2 for the fall stops.** Its ring and plume don't go in. The burn's part goes ahead.
- **Two falls, one language.** A foe returns to the Void. Our fighter isn't destroyed: it goes back to its shard.

## Scope

- **In the block:** the two falls, at a foe's size and a fighter's. The burst sound stays as it is.
- **Waits:** a new sound, the Hard tint (once the round 3 Shade is picked), and the keeper's fall.
- **No gore.** Shards, smoke and light only: no pieces of a body and nothing red.
