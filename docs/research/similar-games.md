## The finding

The director, 2026-11-09: "there is 0 roblox games like this so we are likely touching gold mine". Two corrections and one confirmation:

- **Games with this name's shape exist.** Four were found, and none has players.
- **The loop of the chicken game has already been reskinned with anime fighters, twice, in October 2026. Both sit at zero players.** So the theme on that loop, by itself, hasn't drawn anyone.
- **Nothing found combines** a restocking shop, a base, anime-style fighters and automatic fights. That gap is real. What would fill it is the making, starting with the first minute.

## The live template

**Grow a Chicken Fighter** (2026-06-16): 123.9 million visits, about 1,200 playing, peak 242,669 on 2026-08-22 (sourced [32]). Hatch eggs, send chickens to a pit for cash, climb a boss tower, fuse two into a mutated one, rebirth, eggs that progress offline, an arena of three against three that fights by itself (sourced [34], a snippet). No restocking shop found. Other reskins of it: a dragon one (1.4 million visits) and a dinosaur one (455,000).
