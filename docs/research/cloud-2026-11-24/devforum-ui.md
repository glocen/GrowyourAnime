# Roblox UI/UX craft: the useful tips from the DevForum and the Creator docs

Why this matters for us: `docs/ui.md` sets phone-first landscape, quiet HUD, premium motion and one component library. Several Roblox features from 2025-2026 (Styling, PreferredInput, ViewportDisplaySize, Styling Transitions) fit those rules directly, and two of our numbers (text 14 px, haptics on every press) collide with what Roblox staff say.

Research helper, 2026-11-24. Labels: SOURCED = a Roblox doc or staff post says it; COMMUNITY = experienced devs agree on the forum; OPINION = this note's judgment. Access: the DevForum's HTML pages came back empty to the fetch tool, so forum posts were read through their public Discourse `.json` form (same text, with dates, authors and like counts). A batched `curl` download was refused by this session's permission check, not by the host. Nothing was signed into or paid for.

## A. Screen architecture and navigation

1. Roblox's UI designer names four placements: HUD (persistent, at the edges), popup/modal (a quick decision), full-screen dialog (pauses play, such as customisation), and toast (brief, non-blocking). Keep each kind behaving the same everywhere. [devforum.roblox.com/t/3074034](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3074034) (uiuxartist, Engine UI team, 2024-07, 228 likes). SOURCED.
   For us: the dock is HUD, Home is a full-screen dialog, a confirm is a modal, the event chips and "shards: into the Breaker" are toasts. Write this mapping into the component library so no screen invents a fifth kind.
2. Use progressive disclosure, and show only the controls the current context needs. Minimise steps to a goal and map flows to find extra friction. [create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/ui-ux-design](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/ui-ux-design). SOURCED.
   For us: this backs Home's cards, each opening one whole screen with its pages inside. Count taps for the five most frequent jobs (equip, break a shard, assign a worker, claim, settings) and keep each at 3 taps or fewer from the world (OPINION as a target).
3. Lean on conventions players already know: an X to close, grey for unusable, a lock for locked, red for an unaffordable price. [ui-ux-design doc](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/ui-ux-design). SOURCED.
   For us: function is free, so the X, lock and grey states stay recognisable, but drawn in our shard-cut kit. Rule 4 ("a disabled button says why") goes one step past the convention: keep it.
4. Navigation depth: the docs give no limit. OPINION: two levels (screen, then a page or tab inside it) plus at most one modal on top. "Back" always closes the topmost layer: the modal, then the screen, then nothing. Closing is faster than opening (already in our rule 5).
   For us: one back stack in the UI controller, driven by the on-screen X, gamepad B and a keyboard key. Check in Studio which key we can actually bind, because Roblox reserves Esc for its own menu.

## B. Phone first: safe areas, thumbs, scaling

5. `ScreenGui.ScreenInsets` defaults to `CoreUISafeInsets`, which keeps content clear of the notch and Roblox's top-bar buttons. `DeviceSafeInsets` avoids only hardware cutouts, and `None` is full-bleed. [ScreenInsets enum](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/enums/ScreenInsets), [Notched screen support](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/notched-screen-support-full-release/2074324). SOURCED.
   For us: two layers. A full-bleed backdrop layer (`None`) holds the dim and screen backgrounds. The content layer keeps `CoreUISafeInsets`. Never put a tappable thing in the backdrop layer.
6. Roblox's top bar grew from 36 to 58 px with Experience Controls. `GuiService.TopbarInset` gives the free area under it, and it can read zero on the first frame, so wait for a non-zero value. Xbox reports 0. [Experience Controls beta](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/studio-beta-for-experience-controls-available-now/2567355), [inset bug](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guiservicegetguiinset-returns-0-0-0-0-for-the-first-frame-of-the-game-with-new-topbar-ui/2973508). SOURCED (the 58 is staff's; later reports differ by device, which is COMMUNITY).
   For us: the lead's card (top left) and the pills (top right) sit in the top band. Position them from `TopbarInset` and re-read it on change, never from a hard-coded 36 or 58.
7. Thumb zones: on phones the default controls take the bottom-left (stick) and bottom-right (jump) corners, so keep information and buttons out of both. Place custom action buttons relative to the jump button. A button 40% down from the top is reachable on a phone and "almost unreachable" on a tablet. [Position and size](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/position-and-size). SOURCED. Never put buttons in the stick zone, where a dynamic thumbstick drag lands on them by mistake. [Correct way to design mobile buttons](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-correct-way-to-design-mobile-buttons/2494558) (412 likes). COMMUNITY.
   For us: the bottom-centre dock is safe. TR52's question (the signature ring in the top left) is real: on a tablet the top left is the hardest corner to reach. Measure it in the tablet class too, not only on a phone.
8. Offset pixels are device-independent: Roblox normalises a phone's resolution (a 2436x1125 iPhone is treated as 812x375, about 160 DPI), so a fixed Offset is roughly the same physical size everywhere. Staff warn that pure-Scale UI turns unreadably small on phones and that Scale is hard across aspect ratios. [UI scaling best practices](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/specific-details-about-ui-scaling-best-practices/3888004) (quoting Tiffblocks, Roblox staff). SOURCED. Roblox's UI designer recommends the opposite (Scale plus `UIAspectRatioConstraint`, Scale-based `UICorner`). [3074034](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3074034). SOURCED, so staff disagree.
   For us (OPINION): build components at Offset sizes from size tokens, so a 44 px target really is 44 everywhere, and let layouts (lists, flex, two or three panes) use Scale to fill the screen. That gives "fixed pieces, fluid layout", which matches our three layout classes.
9. `UIScale` scales a subtree, its `UIStroke` and `UICorner` included. The docs' own uses are design zoom and a hover pop. [Size modifiers](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/size-modifiers). SOURCED.
   For us: use `UIScale` for the press sink (0.94) and the payoff pop, never `Size` or `TextSize` (tip 22). One `UIScale` per layout class at the root can also nudge the whole kit, but do that only if the tokens can't.
10. `GuiService.ViewportDisplaySize` (full release 2025-10) returns Small, Medium or Large by the screen's physical size, not its pixel count. Small covers phones, tablets and handhelds. Staff say not to use it for rendering-quality decisions. [3880384](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/full-release-build-cross-platform-ui-with-the-viewportdisplaysize-api/3880384) (missmandypanda, staff). SOURCED.
    For us: it gives "detected, never asked" for free, but Small lumps phone and tablet together. Our compact and regular classes still need the viewport's size inside Small. Desktop is Medium, a TV is Large.
11. `UserInputService.PreferredInput` (full release 2025-10) is KeyboardAndMouse, Gamepad or Touch, and it changes live. Use it to switch prompts and layouts instead of `TouchEnabled`, which is now true on touch laptops. [3750890](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/full-release-introducing-preferredinput-and-improved-touch-capabilities/3750890) (staff). SOURCED.
    For us: this is our input detection. One signal drives hover glows (mouse only), the focus ring (gamepad) and the 44 px touch sizing.
12. Touch target size: Roblox's docs give no number. The example code sizes a custom button at 0.8 of the jump button. [Position and size](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/position-and-size). SOURCED. Outside Roblox: Apple uses 44 pt, Android 48 dp (about 9 mm), and NN/g about 1 cm. [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/touch-target-size/). SOURCED, not Roblox.
    For us: our 44 px rule is sound, given the normalised px of tip 8. Make the dock and primary buttons 48 or more, and let the hit area be larger than the drawn shard shape. Shard-cut corners shrink the visible target.
13. Test in Studio's Device Emulator, phone presets first, plus the "Generic Handheld HD" preset for handhelds. [3074034](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3074034), [Handheld ready](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/handheld-ready-optimize-your-creations-for-any-device/3887726). SOURCED. Edit mode and play mode show different safe-area bounds because of the top bar. [2074324](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/notched-screen-support-full-release/2074324). COMMUNITY.
    For us: judge layout only in a playtest capture, never from edit mode. This already fits the `/ga-studio-test` habit.

## C. Text and numbers

14. Roblox staff: "sizes of 20 or above are readable no matter what device you're on", thanks to DPI normalisation. Use few fonts and few sizes. [All about text](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/all-about-text-best-practices/1515743) (staff, 2021, updated 2023). The docs say never go below 9. [Size modifiers](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/size-modifiers). SOURCED.
    For us: **our rule 1 sets text at 14 px or more, well under staff's 20.** OPINION: 20 is the floor for anything read during a fight (health text, damage, pills), 16 to 18 for secondary labels in a still screen, and 14 only for captions. This needs research's call and AR12's font tried at those sizes on a phone capture.
15. Avoid `TextScaled` where you can. It takes about 10x longer to lay out, it makes many unique sizes that strain the font atlas, and the size jumps as the text changes. Use fixed sizes with `AutomaticSize` instead. [1515743](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/all-about-text-best-practices/1515743). SOURCED. Where you keep it, cap it with `UITextSizeConstraint`. COMMUNITY.
    For us: no `TextScaled` in the kit. Text sizes come from tokens per layout class.
16. Players can set Text Size (`GuiService.PreferredTextSize`, live for everyone since 2025-08). It enlarges all text except `TextScaled` text and text capped by `UITextSizeConstraint.MaxTextSize`. Design with `AutomaticSize` and `TextWrapped`. [Text scaling setting](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-text-scaling-setting-studio-beta/3136967), [Accessibility](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/accessibility). SOURCED.
    For us: test every screen at "Largest". Fixed-width pills and damage digits may need a `MaxTextSize` cap, but labels in screens should grow.
17. Abbreviations come from community modules. The common pattern is a suffix table with rounding, and some modules wrap ICU for locale grouping. [FormatNumber](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/527979). COMMUNITY. Many simple snippets round up or mishandle decimals.
    For us: we already decided "three figures, cut, never rounded up" (`Rules.Hud.Short`). Keep it in pure Luau with logic cases at the edges (99,999 / 100,000 / 999,999 / 1,000,000).
18. Count-ups and changing digits: change `Text` at most once per frame, and hold the label at a fixed width so the pill doesn't jiggle as digits change width. OPINION (from tips 15 and 22). Use `MaxVisibleGraphemes` for reveal effects, not `string.sub` each frame. [1515743](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/all-about-text-best-practices/1515743). SOURCED.
    For us: Credits and CR count-ups (rule 4's "count-up shows the real change") get a fixed-width slot. Prefer a font whose digits are equal width (AR12's pick).
19. Fonts: set `FontFace = Font.new(family, weight)`, since weight only exists on `FontFace`. `ContentProvider:PreloadAsync` does not reliably guarantee a font is ready. [Font](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/datatypes/Font) (SOURCED), [preload issue](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/gettextboundsasync-claims-preloadasync-can-be-used-to-make-sure-font-is-loaded-this-is-inaccurate/2984263) (COMMUNITY).
    For us: load AR12's face behind the loading screen and check it with a text-bounds measurement before the HUD first fades in, or the refont flashes the default face.

## D. Hierarchy, colour, glass

20. Direct attention with colour, size, negative space, proximity and motion: bright for what matters, muted for the rest. Use motion sparingly. One colour means one thing everywhere (their example: health is green in text, icon and bar). [ui-ux-design doc](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/ui-ux-design). SOURCED. Never carry information by colour alone (over 5% of players have some colour blindness). [Accessibility](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/accessibility). SOURCED.
    For us: this confirms "words wear their meaning's colour" plus the rarity's frame shape. Audit that no rarity, element or type edge reads by hue alone.
21. Players can set Background Transparency (`GuiService.PreferredTransparency`, 1 is the default and 0 is opaque). Staff say to multiply a panel's default background transparency by it, and to listen for changes. [Accessibility settings](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-accessibility-settings/2723187) (staff, 2023). SOURCED.
    For us: our dark glass panels must honour it. Put it in the panel component once, and text stays readable over a bright festival ground.
    There is no true UI blur: Lighting's `BlurEffect` blurs the whole world behind all UI, costs GPU on phones, and is cut on low graphics. [UI blur threads](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/blur-effects-for-ui-elements/359498). COMMUNITY. For us: get "glass" from the panel's own gradient, edge and dim, not from blur.

## E. Motion

22. Don't tween `TextSize` (it makes a new font size every frame) or a text `UIStroke`'s `Thickness` (the engine renders and stores many glyph sizes, causing lag or flicker). [1515743](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/all-about-text-best-practices/1515743), [Appearance modifiers](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/appearance-modifiers). SOURCED.
    For us: the damage-number pop and the rarity "living edge" animate `UIScale`, colour and transparency, never `TextSize` or stroke thickness on text.
23. Easing: the default is Quad Out. Back overshoots slightly. Bounce and Elastic overshoot repeatedly. Roblox's own button tutorial uses 0.15 s for press-in and pop-out. [UI animation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/animation). SOURCED. Use Out for deceleration, tween each property separately with its own timing, and give larger elements longer times. Back with InOut looks wrong. [Button fluidity](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-i-achieve-this-button-fluidity/2274415), [Easing reference](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ui-easing-reference/958037). COMMUNITY.
    For us: a starting tune for `Config.Hud` Motion is press-in about 0.06 to 0.08 s Quad Out to 0.94, release about 0.15 to 0.2 s Back Out, and screen close about 0.7 of open (OPINION; tuned live, as rule 5 says).
24. Styling Transitions (full release 2026-07-27) tween style changes natively, hover and press GuiStates included, with TweenInfo-style settings. Staff call this better than Luau tweening, and per-property transitions perform better. Transitions fire only on style changes (a tag or GuiState), not on layout changes. [4646870](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/full-release-styling-transitions/4646870) (staff). SOURCED. The post doesn't say whether they respect Reduced Motion.
    For us: hover glow and press sink can live in the stylesheet, with no per-button script. Verify a Reduced Motion swap still works (tip 25) before relying on it.
25. Reduced Motion: `GuiService.ReducedMotionEnabled`. Staff patterns are to turn fly-ins into fades (`CanvasGroup.GroupTransparency`), or set the tween time to 0, and to stop or replace looping motion. [2723187](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-accessibility-settings/2723187), [Accessibility](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/accessibility). SOURCED.
    For us: rule 5's "Reduced Motion turns moves into fades" has a real API. The Epic and Legendary shine loops must stop under it, and the screen shake on a failure must too.
26. Haptics: `HapticEffect` (full release 2025-09) has UIClick, UIHover and UINotification presets, plus `ClickHaptic` and `HoverHaptic` on buttons. Staff list console and VR controllers. Controllers on phones are not yet supported, intensity below 0.1 may do nothing, and "not all devices vibrate". [3660577](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/full-release-you-can-now-publish-haptic-effects-in-your-experience/3660577). SOURCED.
    For us: rule 5's "a haptic on every press" may do nothing on most phones. The post doesn't confirm a phone's own vibration. Test on the director's phone, and never let the haptic carry feedback that sound and motion don't.

## F. Performance

27. Static UI is cached until something under its ScreenGui changes, and any change re-renders that whole ScreenGui. [Static UI performance](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/static-ui-performance-improvements/222557) (staff, 2019: old, and the post said it might become more granular). SOURCED but dated. Forum users disagree on one ScreenGui versus many. [GUI optimization tips](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/gui-optimization-tips/3986563). COMMUNITY.
    For us (OPINION): split ScreenGuis by how often they change: a static HUD frame, a live layer (bars, ring, count-ups, chips), the open screen, and toasts. A looping rarity shine then doesn't redraw the whole HUD.
28. CanvasGroup: the docs' tool for fading a group. On the forum it costs texture memory, keeps that memory after `Visible = false`, renders blurry on graphics levels 1 to 4, can be down-scaled under memory pressure, and can show white or blank on low-memory phones. No staff fix has been posted (2025-2026). [3502537](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/using-many-canvasgroups-is-unworkable-until-proper-memory-management-methods-are-implemented/3502537), [3986563](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/gui-optimization-tips/3986563). COMMUNITY.
    For us: use at most one live CanvasGroup, for the screen that is opening or closing. Never nest them, never keep hidden ones parked, and fade simple panels by their own transparency. Test a screen open at graphics level 1 on a phone.
29. Images: texture memory follows pixel count. Most minor images should be under 256x256, and 512x512 is usually the ceiling unless the image fills much of the screen. Use sprite sheets (`ImageRectOffset`/`ImageRectSize`) and preload only key menu images. [Improve performance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/improve). SOURCED. Pad sheet tiles by about 1 px against bleeding. COMMUNITY.
    For us: AR3's frames and icons should be 9-slice ([9-slice](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/9-slice)) and atlas sheets sized to these numbers. Keep gear icons at 256 or smaller.
30. Frames, `UICorner` and `UIGradient` load no asset and are cheap. Sliced ImageLabels with `UICorner` produce artifacts. [3986563](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/gui-optimization-tips/3986563). COMMUNITY. Strokes and corners are fine. Tweening stroke thickness on text is the cost (tip 22).
    For us: the shard-cut corner is an image (a 9-slice), not `UICorner`. Don't stack both on one element.
31. Big lists: hundreds of cells in a ScrollingFrame drop FPS even when hidden, and creating or destroying them causes spikes. The fix is virtualisation (render only the visible cells, reuse them). [1000+ frames](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-i-optimize-a-scrolling-frame-for-over-1000-frames/952022), [virtual list](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/virtual-scrolling-list-virtualization/2732175). COMMUNITY. Use `AutomaticCanvasSize`, not `AutomaticSize`, on a ScrollingFrame. [Size modifiers](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/size-modifiers). SOURCED.
    For us: the bag and the Voidkin list pool their cells from day one, even with small counts, so growth never forces a rewrite. Set `Parent` last when building a cell.
32. Flex layout (`UIListLayout` Wraps, Horizontal/VerticalFlex plus `UIFlexItem` Grow, Shrink, Fill; live on all platforms since 2024-08): staff recommend fewer than 1,000 items. Some combinations with `AutomaticSize` are unsupported (non-zero anchors, scaled size or padding, `TextScaled`). [3096190](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/flex-features-for-uilistlayout-client-release/3096190) (staff). SOURCED.
    For us: flex makes the dock, the pill row and the two- or three-pane screens reflow per class without code. There is no `UIFlexLayout` class, so search the docs for "flex" under UIListLayout.
33. Watch for growth in the Developer Console's InstanceCount (leaked UI), and tween UI on the client only. [Improve performance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/improve). SOURCED. Ctrl+F2 (Shift+Ctrl+F2 in Studio) shows layout counts and render items. [1515743](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/all-about-text-best-practices/1515743). SOURCED.
    For us: add an "open and close every screen 20 times, InstanceCount is flat" check to the UI self-test.

## G. Controller and keyboard

34. Gamepad focus: on opening a menu call `GuiService:Select(container)` and set the default with `SelectionOrder` (lower first). Mark a modal `SelectionGroup = true` with `SelectionBehavior* = Stop` so focus can't drift to the HUD. Listen to `SelectionChanged` to recover when a focused cell is destroyed. [Gamepad UI selection APIs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-gamepad-ui-selection-apis/1791278) (staff, 2022). SOURCED. A is accept and B is cancel. [Gamepad input](https://create.roblox.com/docs/input/gamepad). SOURCED.
    For us: this is exactly our "A selects, B goes back". Recovery on destroy matters for the bag when an item is sold or fused.
35. Hotkey hints: `InputActionLabel` shows an Input Action's binding for the current device automatically, but it is Studio Beta and can't be published (2026-08). `PreferredBinding` and `DisplayName`/`DisplayImage` on bindings are live. Roblox now urges the Input Action System over ContextActionService. [4779420](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/studio-beta-no-code-hotkey-hints-with-inputactionlabel/4779420), [IAS release](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/full-release-input-action-system-ias-newly-converted-player-scripts/4678416). SOURCED.
    For us: bind UI actions (tab left and right on the shoulders, back, confirm) as Input Actions now, and draw our own hint glyphs from `PreferredInput` until the label ships.

## H. New tools worth knowing (2024-2026)

36. UI Styling is fully released (2026-01-20). StyleSheet, StyleRule, StyleLink and StyleDerive give CSS-like global overrides, with tokens and themes. StyleQuery (2026-05) adds queries on `ViewportDisplaySize` and `PreferredInput`. Limits: no styling of several pseudo-instances (for example two UIStrokes) unless each has its own name or tag. [4275082](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/full-release-ui-styling-is-officially-released/4275082), [StyleQuery](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/full-release-stylequery-more-styling-features/4566519), [Styling doc](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/styling) (staff). SOURCED.
    For us: this is the native form of "one library, size tokens per class" and "four palettes from one kit". Each palette is a theme, each layout class a query, and components carry tags. The builder should weigh it before writing a home-made token system.
37. `UIDragDetector` (fully released 2025-12-03) gives sliders and draggable UI with little code. Hover events misfire during a drag, and a detector inside a button blocks its click. [3109263](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-uidragdetectors-released/3109263) (staff). SOURCED.
    For us: use it for the Settings sliders (music, sound, effects). Don't use it inside slot buttons. Drag-to-equip is a design question, not a default.
38. `UIStroke` gained `StrokeSizingMode` (ScaledSize), `BorderStrokePosition` (Inner, Center, Outer), `BorderOffset` and `ZIndex`. [Appearance modifiers](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/appearance-modifiers), [beta post](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/studio-beta-uistroke-improvements-scaling-offsets-and-more/3958036). SOURCED. Whether it is fully out of beta isn't confirmed here.
    For us: an Outer stroke with an offset gives a glow edge with no image. Check its status in Studio before AR3 relies on it.

## Top 5 to act on

1. **Raise the text floor:** rule 1's 14 px is under Roblox staff's "20 readable on any device". Research to decide: 20 for anything read mid-fight, 16 to 18 secondary, 14 captions only (tip 14). Test at Text Size "Largest" (tip 16).
2. **Build the kit on UI Styling:** palettes as themes, layout classes as StyleQueries on `ViewportDisplaySize` plus the viewport size, hover and press as Styling Transitions. One native system instead of our own (tips 10, 11, 24, 36).
3. **Motion that's safe and cheap:** animate `UIScale`, colour and transparency only, never `TextSize` or stroke thickness on text. One CanvasGroup at a time. `ReducedMotionEnabled` stops the shine loops and the shake (tips 22, 25, 28).
4. **Respect the edges:** a backdrop layer at `None`, content at `CoreUISafeInsets`, the top band from `TopbarInset`, both bottom corners empty, and TR52's top-left ring also measured on a tablet (tips 5, 6, 7).
5. **Don't trust phone haptics:** they may not fire on phones. Sound and motion carry every press, and the haptic is a bonus (tip 26). Pair with `PreferredTransparency` on glass panels (tip 21).
