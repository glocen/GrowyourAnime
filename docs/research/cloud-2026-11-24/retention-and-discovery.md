# Retention and discovery for a game like ours (research helper, 2026-11-24)

**Why it matters.** Roblox's Home starts over 90% of platform traffic, and since June 2026 it ranks games by how many of their players come back over 28 days, not by who wins the click.
So the first three minutes, the reasons to return on days 2 to 28, and play with friends now decide reach more than any feature count.

**How this was read.** Web search plus page fetches, 2026-11-24. Labels: **SOURCED** (Roblox's own docs or staff, or a named data report), **COMMUNITY** (developers' threads, press, one studio's account), **OPINION** (an analyst's or our own inference). DevForum topic pages came back empty to the fetcher. Their Discourse `.json` form read fine, so the staff posts below were read in full. A shell `curl` was refused by this session's sandbox (not by the host). Vendor blogs that quote unaudited retention numbers (RoLearn, RoWatcher, Endsights) were seen in results and left out. Nothing here was tested by us.

## Findings

### A. How Home picks games now

1. **The signals since 2026-06-15.** *Most important:* play-through rate (plays per RFY impression), first-play bounce rate (leaving under 60 s, and from 61 to 180 s, a negative signal), play days per user and playtime per user, each on Day 1, Days 2 to 7 and Days 8 to 28. *Important:* intentional co-play days, qualified play sessions, spend days, Robux per user. Playtime counts only up to 60 minutes per user per game per day. Weights are not published. SOURCED: [Discovery docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery), [staff post, 2026-06-15](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/4684575), [Roblox newsroom, June 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox).
   For us: what counts is the value of a session, not its length. A 10 to 15 minute run plus the base fits well under the cap. Under these signals our weakest spot is the window from 61 to 180 s: arriving, picking a shard, the reveal, and the walk or trip to the first fight.
2. **Averages per user, organic users only, cohorts first.** The signals are averages per player, so a small game isn't penalised for being small. Only players who came from RFY count, and players bought with ads are left out of ranking. A new game is tried on small groups of new players and widened if they stay. It doesn't wait 28 days: the first signals are play-through and bounce. A 50% gain can still bring little extra reach if rivals improved more. SOURCED: [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq).
   For us: the first cohorts set our fate, and an "Early Access" label doesn't shield them. Don't open to the public with a build whose first three minutes we haven't measured.
3. **OUTDATED: "qualified play-through rate" (qPTR) and the 7-day window.** These were the 2025 signals ([staff, 2025-03-31](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3587441)) and were removed on 2026-06-15. Guides that tell you to chase qPTR, including the Roblox executive's signal list in [Deconstructor of Fun, April 2026](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/how-to-build-a-real-business-on-roblox-in-2026), predate the change. One detail from the 2025 post still holds as of its December 2025 edit: play sessions in reserved servers count as intentional co-play.
4. **Thumbnails.** Home won't show a game with only the default thumbnail. In Roblox's own test, thumbnail personalization (a multi-armed bandit across your set) raised qPTR by 8.5% on average. Staff advise waiting 7 days or until the next update between changes. Accurate thumbnails cut bounces, and visuals or titles that look nearly identical to another game's lower discoverability. Roblox says it will test running personalization on the new play-through rate. SOURCED: [launch post](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/live-now-personalize-your-thumbnails-to-attract-more-users/3257233), [staff tips](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/5-tips-from-roblox-staff-to-get-the-most-out-of-thumbnail-personalization/3471689), [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq). The +8.5% was measured on the old metric.
   For us: "a screenshot of a reveal looks like an advert" (`docs/north-star.md`) is the right instinct. It needs to be its own piece: three to five truthful frames taken in the game.
5. **NEW, and missing from our docs: the Kids and Select gate (2026).** A new game is shown only to age-checked players 16 and over. It reaches Roblox Kids (ages 5 to 8) and Select (ages 9 to 15) only after **100 unique plays by "highly engaged" age-checked players within 60 days**. Such a player meets tenure, playtime and spend tests, and the spend can be a purchase anywhere on Roblox. The creator, a group's owner included, must be ID- or age-verified with 2-Step Verification on, and must either pay a **refundable** per-game publishing fee or keep Roblox Plus or Premium for two months. A paid expedited review exists (50,000 Robux, refundable). Kids sees only games rated Minimal or Mild. Select sees Minimal, Mild and Moderate. SOURCED: [Kids and Select docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select), [content maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity). One DevForum summary says 500 plays and a 1,000 Robux fee. The docs say 100 and don't state the fee, so the docs win and the fee is unverified.
   For us: this is the largest discovery gate we face, and both of its routes cost money, so it falls under rule 1 and is a tier-3 call for the director. To keep Kids open, a "slightly mature" tone has to stay at a Mild rating. Moderate still reaches Select.

### B. The first session

6. **Roblox's own FTUE advice.** New players "decide their interest in a game within minutes." Get them to the fun within about 5 minutes, using short tooltips rather than long tutorials. Teach both what to do and why. Keep the first experience thresholds low and tune them live with Configs. End onboarding with a "moment of joy". Keep short, mid and long goals visible. SOURCED: [onboarding](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding), [retention](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention).
   For us: the north star's seven beats already follow this, and the Choose beat is the moment of joy.
7. **Funnels find the leak; fix it before adding content.** In Roblox's worked example, 70% of new players were lost between step 1 and step 2. The fix was an icon over the pots. The post advises prioritising drop-off fixes over content updates. The "new user first-session retention" chart shows the share of new players still in the game X minutes after joining. SOURCED: [DevRel, 2024](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3064458), [analytics post, 2024](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/2890832).
   For us: we already log the eight beats through `LogOnboardingFunnelStepEvent` (`docs/test-requests.md`). That's a strength. Read the biggest drop once real players arrive.
8. **Teleports and loading inside the bounce window.** A staff reply says the ranking metrics are counted for the whole experience, so a teleport by itself doesn't hurt ranking. COMMUNITY, from a search snippet of [this thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/average-visit-length-doesnt-account-for-teleports/1416125?page=2). The trip still costs time: about 5 to 6 s for a new server (staff, 2023), stalls of 60 s and more have been reported, and ProfileStore's hand-off can add about 5 s (all in `docs/research/game-mode-places.md`). A commenter adds that low-memory phones crashing early count as bounces (COMMUNITY).
   For us, OPINION: if the first fight needs a trip to the Festival place, that trip lands in the 61 to 180 s bucket. Measure it before deciding anything.
9. **What the hits do in the first seconds.** You start on your own plot, the first verb is a single input, a payout comes within seconds, and income turns passive at once (SOURCED, our `docs/research/first-seconds.md`). Naavik credits Grow a Garden's "gentle first-time user experience" and its idle timers that bring players back, and notes that Roblox hits turn menu mechanics into things players "walk through, show off, and do together". OPINION, an analyst: [Naavik, 2025-07-06](https://naavik.co/digest/predicting-the-next-big-hits-on-roblox/).

### C. Retention numbers and levers

10. **Benchmarks, so we judge our own numbers honestly.** In GameAnalytics' 2026 data (500+ Roblox games with over 1M MAU each, Aug 2025 to Jul 2026), the median game keeps D1 **10.3%**, D7 **1.6%** and D30 **0.5%**. The top 1% keep **22.2 / 9.1 / 4.7%**. GA calls the common "20% D1 is good, 40% excellent" figures inflated. In its 2025 data, games whose median session runs 19 to 24 minutes have a median D1 of 11.5% and a 90th-percentile D1 of 18.2%, against 4.3% for 0 to 3 minute games. SOURCED, third party, covering only GA-integrated large games, and the 2026 figures read through a [summary](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-key-roblox-and-roblox): [GA 2025 report](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2025-roblox-report). These are correlations, not causes.
    For us: "comes back the next day" needs a number. A D1 near 15% for players who finish Lantern Row would be a good result. That figure is our target, not a prediction.
11. **Roblox's levers for each day.** D1: a clear core loop, a short FTUE, performance across devices. D7: short and long goals, variety, difficulty that neither stalls nor runs out. D30: small updates to existing mechanics every **2 to 4 weeks** and larger features every **2 to 3 months**, plus social systems (trading, parties, guilds, PvP, leaderboards) to carry players between updates. SOURCED: [retention docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention).
12. **What keeps players past a month.** High D30 "almost always" belongs to games with a big IP or deep long-term progression (GA, SOURCED via the summary above). We have no IP, so our D30 rests on the build's depth, on the roster, and on how often we update.
13. **Appointments while away.** Grow a Garden's crops grow offline, which gives players a reason to return (OPINION, [Substack](https://jens.substack.com/p/gamification-of-user-engagement); Naavik, above). GameRefinery treats appointment mechanics and login calendars as standard tools in mobile games (COMMUNITY, [GameRefinery](https://www.gamerefinery.com/keep-your-players-in-game-with-appointment-mechanics/)).
    For us: the battery, the Condenser and the Training machine all working while the player is away make a strong appointment. The battery's cap is the natural "come back" moment.
14. **Daily goals and streaks: little hard evidence.** Roblox recommends visible goals but publishes nothing comparing streaks with banked dailies. Mobile games commonly reset a broken streak or let players buy it back (COMMUNITY, [ironSource](https://medium.com/ironsource-levelup/keeping-players-engaged-common-strategies-for-daily-login-by-top-studios-b20d9b595035)).
    For us, OPINION: "bank up to three days, no streak" is a fair and humane choice, and the evidence is neutral on it. Since play days per user is a top signal, each day still needs a fresh reason to log in. Today's three, the battery and the shop's featured stock do that, but only if players can see them without opening the game (see 16).

### D. Playing together

15. **Co-play is counted and lasts longer.** Intentional co-play days is a ranking signal. Roblox's own data shows co-play sessions run 1.9 times longer than solo ones (Q2 2024). Since the December 2025 edit, sessions in reserved servers count as co-play. The friend referral system (2025) passes `ReferredByPlayerId` in the join data and lets a game reward both sides. SOURCED: [referral, 2025-04-23](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3623795), [referral docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/referral-system), [staff, 2025-03-31](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3587441).
    For us: shared packs, joinable elites and a neighbourhood of eight are good co-play by proximity. Parties are out of launch, though, and the Festival is a place of its own, so two friends can end up in different servers.
16. **Experience notifications.** Players opt in, 13 and over only, at most one a day. They should be personal and give the player something to act on now. A notification must never be required to progress, never generic advertising, and never a dark pattern. Delivery isn't guaranteed: reach grows with how much players engage. The opt-in prompt is Roblox's own, can't be reworded, and shows at most once every 30 days. SOURCED: [docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-notifications).
    For us: "your battery is full" is exactly the case the docs describe. It's already proposed and waits on the director (`docs/test-requests.md`).

### E. Updates and live ops

17. **Cadence.** WonderWorks shipped a game in six weeks with four people, then 52 consecutive weekly updates, and its CEO said being "in the 90th percentile across all those metrics" means a hit (COMMUNITY, one studio's account, in [DoF, 2026](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/how-to-build-a-real-business-on-roblox-in-2026)). Grow a Garden updated about weekly through its growth, then apologised publicly for "repetitive" updates (COMMUNITY, [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/robloxs-top-experience-grow-a-garden-is-shifting-into-a-new-era-and-promises-deeper-farming-after-a-series-of-disappointing-updates-i-hope-the-community-can-see-this-change-as-a-positive-attempt-to-keep-the-game-fresh-and-enjoyable-for-everyone)). Roblox puts events on the game's page and in a Trending Events chart, and sends updates to players who opted in (SOURCED, [events docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-events)). The FAQ confirms that updates move the Home signals.
    For us: Roblox's own floor is a small update every 2 to 4 weeks. Weekly is what the leaders do, and repetitive weekly updates have been punished too.

### F. Compliant monetization

18. **Paid random items (page dated 2026-10-15).** The rules cover random items bought with Robux and with any currency Robux buys. Every outcome's odds must show, summing to 100%, before the spend. A labelled "Details" pop-up is enough; a lone (i) icon is not. Odds must update for an outcome a player can only win once. Luck boosts must be explained in numbers before purchase, and the odds must update live while one runs. When `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` is true, the game must offer one of the listed treatments: a free path, a fixed order, a direct buy at expected value, hiding, or blocking. Random rewards earned without currency are exempt. SOURCED: [policy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items).
    For us: `docs/roblox-rules.md` already covers this well. Two lines to check when the store is designed: the live odds for one-time outcomes, and the display of any future luck boost.
19. **Pricing tools cost nothing and pay.** Managed Pricing (2026-06-15) merges Regional Pricing with Price Optimization and enrols new games automatically. Across the top 2,500 games in 2025, Regional Pricing estimated **+4 to 10%** Robux spend and **+1 to 6%** players and playtime. Price Optimization added **+4%** earnings on average. Prices written into code break it. SOURCED: [staff](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/4684738).
    For us: the store's UI should read every price from Roblox, never print a fixed number. No fee is mentioned.
20. **Most players never pay.** Over a year, 3.8% of players spent anything (GA 2026, SOURCED via the summary). Spend days and Robux per user are only "important" signals, below retention.
    For us: an honest store is right, and so is a first session that asks for nothing. A game that sells nothing at all gives up two signals (OPINION).

## Where our plan stands

**Strong, by the evidence:**
- The first session starts on the player's own plot with a reveal as its first beat (6, 9).
- The eight funnel steps are already logged (7).
- The base works while the player is away, with a battery cap that calls them back (13).
- A 10 to 15 minute run gives session value under the 60-minute cap (1).
- No Robux in the first session (20).
- Paid random items are researched to the letter (18).
- Today's three ride on ordinary play, with nothing to lose (14).
- Shared packs and joinable elites give co-play without parties (15).

**Weak, by the evidence:**
- **The Kids and Select gate is in none of our docs (5).** Without it, the game is shown only to verified players 16 and over, a small share of Roblox. Passing it costs a refundable fee or a subscription (rule 1, tier 3), and "slightly mature" must stay at Mild to keep players under 9.
- **The amount of content at launch against D7 and D30 (11, 12, 17).** One ground, five Voidkin and early access: GA says high D30 comes from an IP or deep progression. The build is deep, but players feel its depth only over weeks. No update cadence is decided, and our process (feel line, blockout, review for every piece) has never been sized against a two-to-four-week rhythm. This is the biggest risk to D30, and the plan doesn't name it.
- **Co-play at launch is incidental, not intentional (15).** Parties come last. Nothing makes sure friends who travel to the Festival land in one server, and no invite or referral is planned. Co-play counts on Day 1.
- **The first trip may fall inside the bounce window (1, 8).** The north star's rule "nothing waits more than half a minute" is right, but the teleport to the Festival place has never been timed on a phone.
- **Home is unplanned (4, 16).** No thumbnail or icon piece, no notification, and "How we'll know" has no numbers (10).

## Top 5 to act on

1. **The Kids and Select gate goes to the director now.** Add it to `docs/roblox-rules.md` (research's home). The decision: the refundable fee or a subscription, under the group's owner (rule 1), and aim for a Mild rating in the full questionnaire. Without it, Home shows us only to players 16 and over.
2. **Time the first 180 seconds on a phone before the public opens.** Add timestamps to the funnel's beats. Aim for the reveal inside 60 s and the first hit inside 180 s. If the trip to the Festival breaks that, the first session's first stretch needs a faster route, and choosing it is the director's call (8).
3. **Decide an update cadence and stock it before early access.** At least Roblox's floor of a small update every 2 to 4 weeks. Queue two or three updates' worth of block-first content before opening (Voidkin, the dare pool, Tower seeds), so the premium bar and the cadence don't fight after launch (11, 17).
4. **Make co-play intentional at launch at low cost.** Friends who travel together land in one server. Add Roblox's invite prompt after the first keeper falls, and a referral reward that's earned, never random or sold. These sit inside the director's "parties last" decision, and only the director can bring parties forward (15).
5. **Plan Home as a piece.** Three to five truthful in-game thumbnails with personalization from day one, an icon judged at phone size, and the opt-in battery notification put to the director. Put numbers on "How we'll know": D1 for players who finish Lantern Row, and the first-play bounce under 60 s and from 61 to 180 s (1, 4, 10, 16).
